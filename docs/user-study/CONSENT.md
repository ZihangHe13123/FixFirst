Print one copy per participant. The signed copy stays with the experimenter and is not put in the

**Study**: Evaluating FixFirst, a Python troubleshooting tool
(NUS-ISS Intelligent Reasoning Systems practice module, Group 24)

**研究**：评估 Python 排错工具 FixFirst（NUS-ISS 智能推理系统实践课程，第 24 组）

**What you will do / 你要做什么**
You will try to make the tests of four small, deliberately broken Python projects pass, two of
them with FixFirst and two with your usual tools (a terminal and web search, no AI assistant).
Each task has a 12-minute limit. Afterwards you answer a short questionnaire and two interview
questions. In total about 70 minutes.

你要修 4 个故意弄坏的 Python 小项目，让它们的测试通过：其中两个可以用 FixFirst，另外两个只用你平时的工具（终端和网页搜索，不能用 AI 助手）。每个任务限时 12 分钟。之后填一份简短问卷，回答两个访谈问题。总共大约 70 分钟。

**What is recorded / 记录什么**
Whether and how quickly each task was completed, how often you said "done" before it was, your
own explanation of each problem, and your questionnaire and interview answers. Nothing is recorded
on screen or audio, and your name is not written next to any result.

每个任务是否完成、用了多久，说"好了"但还没修好的次数，你对每个问题原因的解释，以及问卷和访谈的回答。不录屏，不录音，结果旁边不写你的名字。

**How the data are used / 数据怎么用**
Results are stored under a code (P01, P02, …) and used only for this course project's report and
presentation. The anonymous tables may be published with the report. Your name and this form are
kept by the experimenter and deleted after the course.

结果只用编号（P01、P02……）保存，只用于本课程项目的报告和展示；匿名的数据表可能随报告公开。你的名字和这份同意书由实验员保存，课程结束后删除。

**Voluntary / 自愿参加**
Taking part is voluntary. You may stop at any time without giving a reason; your data are then
not used. We are testing the tool, not you.

参加完全自愿。你可以随时停止，不需要说明理由，停止后你的数据不会被使用。我们测试的是工具，不是你。
